# Model Card — RestoAI Complaint Classifier

A model card for the production complaint classifier shipped in
`models/complaints_classifier.joblib` and served by
`src/complaints/classifier.py`. Follows the spirit of Mitchell et al.
(2019). All numbers below come from `results/*.csv` / `results/*.json`
in this repo — every cell points at the file that holds it.

## Model details

- **Model name:** RestoAI Complaint Classifier (TF-IDF + LightGBM-OvR)
- **Version:** 0.6.0 (Day-6 wrap-up, locked 2026-05-16)
- **Architecture:**
  - Feature stack: `TfidfVectorizer(word, 1–2)` ⊕ `TfidfVectorizer(char_wb, 3–5)`,
    `sublinear_tf=True`, `max_features=20k` each (~23k-dim sparse).
  - Head: 8 binary LightGBM-OvR classifiers, one per category.
  - Day-5 Optuna champion hparams (`results/day05_metrics.json`):
    `num_leaves=5, learning_rate=0.116, lambda_l2=4.57,
    num_boost_round=214, min_data_in_leaf=7, scale_pos_weight×=1.58`,
    decision threshold = **0.5** (L3 — NOT the per-class thresholds; see
    "Why L3 not L4" below).
  - Blending: at serving time, the trained head's positive categories
    are union'd with the keyword baseline's. Trained-only mode is
    available via `ComplaintClassifier(blend_with_keyword=False)`.
- **Bundle path:** `models/complaints_classifier.joblib` (~1.6 MB).
- **Training script:** `scripts/day04_train_complaints.py` (Phase-3).
- **Tuning script:** `scripts/day05_phase4_tuning.py` (Optuna, 30 trials).
- **Ablation script:** `scripts/day06_phase5_frontier_ablation.py`.

## Intended use

- **Primary:** Categorise a single restaurant review (free text) into
  zero or more of `{service, food_quality, hygiene, price, delivery,
  portion, ambience, variety}`. Drop-in replacement for
  `manager_system/analyzer.py:categorize_complaints` — the function
  signature is preserved (Hard Rule 5) so the existing Flask routes
  work unchanged.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Flask manager dashboard (`app.py`, port 5000) — via the shim in
    `analyzer.py`.
  - FastAPI service (`api.py`, port 8000) — `POST /complaints`.
  - Streamlit manager dashboard (`app_dashboard.py`, port 8501) — calls
    the FastAPI endpoint.
- **Out of scope:** Non-English reviews (untested), reviews longer than
  ~5k chars (the TF-IDF vocabularies were fit on review-length text),
  cuisine-type detection, automated moderation decisions.

## Training data

- **Source:** 100 reviews drawn from the 5 datasets shipped in
  `datasets/` (zomato.csv, zomato2.csv, mumbaires.csv, Resreviews.csv,
  reviews.csv) — see `data/eval/complaint_eval.csv`.
- **Labels:** Multi-label. Generated by the Day-1 `RICH_PATTERNS` regex
  (`scripts/build_eval_sets.py`). Stratified to ≥8 examples per category.
- **Caveat:** The auto-generated gold shares lexicon with the keyword
  baseline. Day-2 flagged this and Day-6 resolved it: a fresh held-out
  set (`data/eval/complaint_holdout.csv`, 100 rows, seed=2026, disjoint
  from the training pool) was used for the canonical evaluation.

## Performance — Day-6 canonical numbers (fresh 100, `results/ablation.csv`)

| Metric | Trained (L3, t=0.5) | Keyword baseline (L0) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| macro-F1 | **0.8525** | 0.8335 | +0.019 |
| micro-F1 | 0.8458 | 0.8375 | +0.008 |
| subset accuracy | 0.43 | 0.39 | +0.04 |
| latency (CPU, ms/sample) | 34 | 0.1 | (340×) |

### Per-category F1 on the fresh held-out

| Category | L0 keyword | L3 tuned LGBM | Δ | Best |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| service | 0.9895 | 0.9451 | −0.044 | keyword (narrow, unambiguous lexicon) |
| food_quality | 0.7879 | **0.8649** | +0.077 | LGBM |
| hygiene | 0.8571 | 0.8276 | −0.030 | keyword |
| price | 0.9000 | 0.6061 | −0.294 | **keyword (wide margin)** |
| delivery | 0.5373 | **0.9231** | +0.386 | **LGBM (wide margin)** |
| portion | 0.8077 | **0.9268** | +0.119 | LGBM |
| ambience | 0.9315 | 0.7937 | −0.138 | keyword |
| variety | 0.8571 | 0.9091 | +0.052 | LGBM |

**Interpretation.** LGBM wins on broad-context labels (delivery,
food_quality, portion, variety) where the reviewer doesn't use the
literal category name. Keyword wins on narrow-lexicon labels (price,
ambience, service, hygiene) where the literal-stem hit rate is high
(`results/phase2a_lexical_overlap.json` reports service 71.7%, ambience
70.6%, vs food_quality 3.3%, portion 3.8%). The blended-by-default
serving mode picks the union — adopting the LGBM gains without losing
keyword recall on the narrow categories.

## Why L3 (t=0.5), not L4 (per-class thresholds)

Day 5 introduced per-class thresholds (`results/day05_thresholds.json`).
Day 6 measured both on a fresh held-out:

| Layer | orig OOF macro-F1 | fresh held-out macro-F1 |
|---|---|---|
| L3 (t=0.5) | 0.8132 | **0.8525** |
| L4 (per-class thresholds) | **0.8181** | 0.8502 |

L4 beats L3 on the in-sample OOF but loses on the fresh held-out — the
thresholds were grid-searched on OOF probabilities, so they're a
within-pool calibration that doesn't transfer. **L3 is the locked
production config.** For a future revision, recompute thresholds on a
rolling validation slice rather than the training pool.

## Frontier comparison (Day-6, `results/frontier_comparison.csv`)

On the same fresh 100 held-out:

| Strategy | macro-F1 | subset-acc | latency (ms/sample) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trained LGBM (this model) | **0.8502** | **0.43** | 34 | production |
| Keyword baseline | 0.8335 | 0.39 | 0.1 | fallback |
| NLI zero-shot (distilbart-mnli-12-3) | 0.4844 | 0.01 | 1715 | general-purpose stand-in |
| Claude Opus 4.6 | — | — | — | deferred (no API key in autonomous run) |
| GPT-5.4 | — | — | — | deferred |

The multi-label NLI's subset accuracy of 0.01 (1 / 100 reviews) means
generic zero-shot models cannot resolve the 8 overlapping categories at
threshold 0.5 — they over- or under-tag every row.

## Known failure modes

1. **Price under-recall when the literal word is absent.** L3 P=0.444,
   R=0.952 on price → trained head fires too often. Keyword wins this
   category on the held-out (F1 0.900 vs 0.606).
2. **Ambience under-recall on paraphrastic mentions.** "It was very
   cosy" doesn't include "ambience" / "atmosphere" / "decor". Both
   heads miss it, but the trained head loses by 0.14 F1.
3. **Auto-generated labels.** The gold was produced by regex, not human
   annotation. The Day-6 fresh held-out partially mitigates this (it's
   from the same RICH_PATTERNS gold but never seen in training), but a
   future iteration should re-label with humans for high-stakes use.
4. **Length sensitivity.** TF-IDF vocabularies were fit on
   review-length text (~50–500 tokens). Single-sentence input or
   essay-length input is untested.

## Ethical considerations

- **Bias risk:** All reviews are from English-language Indian restaurant
  datasets (zomato / mumbaires). Performance on US/EU restaurant
  reviews or non-English reviews is unmeasured and likely worse.
- **Auto-action risk:** The model is intended for triage / dashboard
  display, not for automated moderation, restaurant penalties, or
  rating changes. The Day-6 subset_acc of 0.43 means 57% of reviews
  are mis-categorised on at least one label — fine for a manager
  dashboard, not fine for an enforcement system.

## Maintenance

- **Retrain trigger:** If the keyword fallback starts outperforming the
  trained head on a new eval set by ≥0.05 macro-F1, retrain.
- **Threshold recalibration:** Recompute per-class thresholds on a
  rolling 30-day held-out slice. Revisit the L3-vs-L4 decision.
- **Drift monitoring:** The Phase-6 RAGAS-proxy log
  (`logs/ragas_proxy.jsonl`) is RAG-only; the complaints endpoint
  should add a similar per-prediction log if used in production.
